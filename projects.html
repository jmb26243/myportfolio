02 / AI RESEARCH & APPLICATION CONCEPT

AI Prompt Lab

Exploring prompt engineering, experimentation, and evaluation with generative AI.

Research website published

PROMPT ENGINEERING EXPERIMENTATION EVALUATION

01. The Problem

Generative AI tools can produce different results depending on how a prompt is written. This project explores how students and beginners can experiment with prompts, evaluate responses, and learn from their results.

02. My Approach

I developed a research website to document the project concept, investigate prompting practices, compare technical approaches, and outline a proposed application.

The planned application would allow users to record prompts, save AI responses, evaluate results, and revisit previous experiments through a private dashboard.
